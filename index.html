Water Ways

Build a pipe line from the village pump to clean water. Read the map, avoid the hazards, and make every pipe count.

How to play

  1. 01Choose a pipe direction to extend your line.
  2. 02The two rows above you reveal hazards; spaces you have visited stay visible.
  3. 03Reach either clean water source before you run out of pipes.
Choose a mode

How scoring works

+10Each move that brings your pipe closer to clean water
−10Each rock hit
+5For every full 10 seconds left after Standard mode
+10For each unused pipe; Challenge bonuses are doubled

Challenge mode doubles time and unused-pipe bonuses and starts with only 60 seconds.
